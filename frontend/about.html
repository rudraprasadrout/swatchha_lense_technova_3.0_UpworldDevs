About Swachh Lens AI Ecosystem

How our multimodal vision pipeline automates municipal waste priority scoring while ensuring citizen privacy protection.

01

YuNet ONNX & Haar Privacy Anonymization

Before photos are processed by AI models or stored in database tables, raw image bytes pass through OpenCV's YuNet ONNX neural face detector and Haar Cascade license plate locator. Regions are pixelated with 20% spatial padding to prevent PII leaks.

02

20m Haversine Spatial Deduplication

When multiple citizens report the same dump site, our spatial deduplication engine queries active tickets within a 20-meter radius. Instead of creating redundant tickets, it increments the existing ticket's duplicate counter and escalates its urgency score.

03

Mistral Pixtral-12B Multimodal Vision Model

Anonymized photo payloads and regional citizen voice notes (in Odia, Hindi, English, etc.) are evaluated by Mistral Pixtral Vision. The model classifies waste categories, estimates volume bands (Small, Medium, Large), and flags drainage or fire risks.

04

Deterministic Algorithmic Priority Scoring

Final score calculation: Score = 2.0 (Base) + Category Weight + Volume Weight + Drain Blockage (+3.0) + Fire Risk (+2.5) + Duplicate Bonus. Capped deterministically between 1.0 and 10.0 to ensure objective municipal truck dispatching.
