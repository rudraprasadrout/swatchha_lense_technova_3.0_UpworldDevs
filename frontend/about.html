SwachhLens System Architecture & AI Workflow

An end-to-end multi-agent computer vision & spatial decision support framework designed for municipal sanitation logistics.

Step 01
Telemetry Ingestion
Captures geo-tagged photographs, GPS coordinates, and regional voice notes in Odia, Hindi, Bengali, or English.
Step 02
Privacy Anonymization
YuNet ONNX neural network and Haar Cascade classifiers detect faces and license plates, pixelating them instantly.
Step 03
Spatial Deduplication
Calculates spherical distance between incoming GPS coordinates and active tickets within a 20m radius.
Step 04
Vision Classification
Pixtral-12B evaluates visual waste severity, categorizing material into Plastic, Organic, E-Waste, or Debris.
Step 05
Dispatch & Routing
Computes urgency score factoring volume, environmental hazards, and location sensitivity for crew dispatch.

Multi-Agent Technical Architecture

SwachhLens decouples civic data collection from municipal response, using lightweight edge anonymization on the frontend and deterministic AI algorithms on the backend.

Pixtral-12B Multimodal Vision AI

Analyzes uploaded imagery to detect waste density, overflow risks, and material composition. Output is structured as key-value tags (Plastic Waste, Heavy Compactor Required, Drain Blockage Risk).

Mistral API Gateway • Vision Transformer

YuNet & Haar Edge Anonymizer

Ensures privacy compliance before storage. The YuNet ONNX deep model detects human faces while Haar Cascades detect vehicle license plates, blurring sensitive regions in memory.

OpenCV Engine • Edge Anonymization

20m Haversine Deduplication

Uses the Great-Circle Haversine formula to compare incoming GPS coordinates against existing active complaints. Prevents ticket spam and recalculates cumulative urgency.

Python Backend • Spatial Distance

Deterministic Dispatch Matrix

Assigns tailored crew units based on AI findings: Manual Sanitation Crew for light waste, Mini Truck + Heavy Compactor for massive piles, and Hazmat Units for chemical/medical hazards.
