20m Haversine Deduplication Engine

Using the Great-Circle Haversine spherical distance algorithm, incoming GPS coordinates are compared against active tickets within a 20m radius. Duplicate reports increment the ticket's confirmation counter (+1) and boost urgency without cluttering the map grid.
